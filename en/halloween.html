Halloween Event in Last Z: Halloween Havoc

The Halloween event in Last Z: Survival Shooter opens from a pumpkin banner and bundles 6 linked mini events: Seven Nights of Terror, Halloween Havoc, Marble Supplies, The Witch's Magic, the Lottery Machine and the Witch's Shop. Here is what each one does and how they connect so you can get the most out of them.

🔗 How the mini events connect

Almost everything revolves around two resources: marbles and wish bottles. Each follows its own path.

The lollipop route

Marbles → Halloween Havoc → Halloween lollipops → Witch's Shop. Marbles come from missions, the pass and the daily free shot.

The ticket route

Wish bottles (The Witch's Magic) → lottery tickets → Lottery Machine → diamonds, lucky numbers and more lollipops.

Seven Nights of Terror works as daily missions that also give you marbles and lollipops, so it feeds both routes.

🌙 Seven Nights of Terror

These are daily missions for 7 days. Day one asks you to bounce marbles (5, 10, 20, 30 and 50), and each mission gives a token, marbles or lollipops, boxes and resources.

Tokens fill the bar at the top, with milestone chests at 5, 20, 50, 70 and 95 tokens. Their prizes include the Dark Night Pumpkin, marbles and orange skill manuals.

🎃 Halloween Havoc (marbles)

This is the main minigame. You spend a marble, pick one of the 3 launchers, and it bounces randomly across the board until it lands in a lollipop bag: the side bags give 30, 50 or 100, and the center one has higher-value prizes.

  • Wish Ring: every hit on it builds progress and pays out its reward when you reach the goal.
  • Multiplier (x5, x10): it launches several marbles in a row and each one resolves on its own, so it saves taps but does not improve your odds.
  • Free shot: the first one each day costs no marbles and resets at 0:00. There is also a daily shot goal that rewards lollipops.

🎟️ Marble Supplies (the pass)

It is a 7-day pass with three reward columns: free, purple and gold. This is what each one adds up to over the 7 days:

🧙 The Witch's Magic (wish bottles)

Every so often the witch gives you magic wish bottles, up to 10 stored. If you gift one to a friend, you get 5 lottery tickets and the receiver gets 1. You can also tick the heart box to send a bottle when you give a "like".

There is also a receiving ranking: every bottle you receive adds 10 points.

🎰 Halloween Lottery Machine

Each ticket you spend gives a random result:

ResultChancePrize
Participation prize75%5 diamonds
Second prize20%20 diamonds
Lucky number5%25 lollipops and a number

Every day at 0:00 (Apocalypse Time) the server draws a number from 1 to 100. If you held that number the previous day, you win the grand prize: 5,000 diamonds, 2 wrenches and 5 one-hour speedups. The last day's prize is sent by mail.

Bottles you receive convert into a maximum of 50 tickets per day.

🛍️ Witch's Shop

This is where you spend Halloween lollipops. Items range from about 2,400 to 60,000 lollipops, some with discounts (-10%, -20% or -30%) and others tagged Returning, meaning they appeared in previous events.

  • Purchase limits: rare rewards have a maximum number of exchanges ("Remaining").
  • Leftover lollipops are lost: unused ones are taken back when the event ends, so spend them in time.

🧠 Quick tips

  • Log in every day: the free shot, the missions and the witch's bottle all reset daily.
  • Gift bottles to your allies and ask them to do the same: everyone's tickets go up.
  • Spend tickets before 0:00 so their numbers enter that night's draw.
  • Buy limited and discounted items first in the shop, and leave the rest for last.
  • Use the multiplier for convenience, not luck: it does not change the odds.

❓ Halloween event FAQ

What is the Halloween event in Last Z?

It is Halloween Havoc, a seasonal event with 6 linked mini events: missions, a marble minigame, a pass, bottle gifting, a lottery and a shop.

Can you play it for free?

Yes. There are missions, a free daily shot, a free column in the pass and the witch's bottles. The purple and gold passes give more marbles and rewards, but they are optional.

What are Halloween lollipops for?

You earn them in the minigame and the lottery, and spend them in the Witch's Shop. Any you don't spend are taken back when the event ends.

When does the Halloween event end?

It starts on different dates depending on your server, so check the countdown inside the event. In our screenshots, the shop closed one day after the other mini events.

Is the pass worth it?

It depends on how much you want to squeeze the shop. The purple pass costs about $5 and the gold one gives many more marbles, wrenches and speedups. If you play for free, you can still follow the missions and the daily shot without any problem.
