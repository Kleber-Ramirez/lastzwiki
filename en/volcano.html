Volcano War in Last Z: Survival Shooter

The Volcano War event in Last Z: Survival Shooter, also known as the Volcano Battle, pits war zones against each other for several days. Servers split into two sides, attackers and defenders, and fight for one hour to score more points than the rival. Here is how its phases work, what each building is worth, what rewards you can win and how to organize your alliance to come out on top.

🗓️ Event phases

The event runs over several days and each one has a clear purpose. The early phases are for forming the sides; the actual combat only lasts an hour.

  1. Day 1AnnouncementThe system shows the groups of war zones taking part. There is nothing to do yet.
  2. Day 2CommunicationPresidents of grouped war zones can agree to cooperate with ungrouped ones. The defender can also activate hard mode.
  3. Days 3 & 4InvitationGrouped war zones formally invite ungrouped ones to join their faction. For example, 594 and 604 can team up against 607 and 602. Each invited zone gets a strategic alliance request showing the prize pool and the proposed loot split, and can accept or decline. Zones that still have no side are assigned at random by the system at 23:55 (Apocalypse Time) on the last invitation day.
  4. Day 5PreparationAllied alliances agree on targets, routes and who defends what before combat.
  5. Day 6BattleOne hour to score more points than the rival faction and take the win.
  6. Days 7 to 10Reward distributionServer presidents hand out the loot and, afterwards, alliance officers can give extra prizes to the people who contributed most. When the stage ends, the system distributes automatically based on the amount stored at that moment.

🔥 Hard mode (hard challenge)

Only the defender can activate it, during day 2. It is a gamble: in exchange for giving up inviting allies in the next phase, the defender gets strong combat bonuses that offset the power gap, and a prize pool that grows gradually.

New: hard mode adds an optional box of exclusive equipment fragments. According to the creator of the video this guide is based on, when it was recorded it was the only free-to-play way to get a hero weapon. That may change in future updates.

🏛️ Buildings & points

Every building on the volcano gives points to your faction and to you, and some have an ability that helps the whole side that controls them.

Lava Furnace8060+20% damage dealt inside the volcano area.
Radiation Warehouse8060+50% destruction value inside the volcano area.

Energy mines give no faction points: they generate personal points while you gather from them.

⭐ How to earn personal points

  • Hold buildings: each one gives its personal share (table above).
  • Gather at the energy mines: there are several levels and the best ones pay more.
  • Defeat enemy troops in the volcano area: you earn points for every block of 10,000 enemy power defeated.

Personal points decide your spot in the individual ranking and whether you qualify for its prizes. An external guide says you need at least 2,000 points to be eligible.

⚔️ Battlefield rules

Where you fight

War zones of the same group travel to the defender's server to contest the buildings and mines.

At the start

All headquarters inside the combat area are kicked out. Use the teleport to get back in.

Free relocation

During the battle you get one free relocation every 5 minutes inside the volcano area, so you can adapt to what is happening.

Time limit

You have one hour. The side with the most faction points when it ends wins.

🎁 Rewards

Prize pool

The total number of chests a side can hand out. For the attacker it doubles (x2) if it successfully invites any war zone and doubles again with a victory. There are four chest types: legendary (gold), epic (blue), rare (copper) and excellent (green).

Allocation chests

Presidents hand them out by merit: the legendary chest goes to someone whose contribution is beyond question, the epic to a key player in the battle, the rare to whoever led the charge and the excellent to whoever reacted fast and fought the enemy. They give hero fragments from the current season (if you are in season 1 you will not get season 2 ones). Gold is the best; the next ones give 15, 10 and 10 fragments, the last with fewer forging stones.

Faction rewards

Every member of the attacking side gets season hero fragment chests, LV3 chests, wood chests and food chests: 20 of each if they win and 10 of each if they lose. In the reference screenshots (July 2026) the fragments were from season 4.

Contribution chests

Valuable resources: warrior medals, forging stones, diamonds and experience chests.

Individual ranking rewards

They are handed out from rank 1 down to 2000. The top 1 and top 2 can win level 7 module boxes, which are very hard to get any other way. There are also orange decoration chests for the faction statues and for decorations with each side's symbol, plus training and healing speedups.

🧠 Strategy to win

  • Defeat enemies, don't just hold buildings. According to community players, it is the easiest way to earn personal points.
  • Hit undefended headquarters. Many players send their best formations to buildings and leave their HQ almost empty: that is your chance.
  • Take the Energy Tower early. Its 10% bonus improves the faction points you earn afterwards.
  • Coordinate the center. Holding the Black Ridge Volcano together with the three buildings that have abilities gives you points and bonuses at once.
  • Use the free relocation. Every 5 minutes you can move to reinforce where people are missing.
  • Plan on day 5. Agree with your alliance who goes to each building before the combat hour starts.

❓ Volcano War FAQ

What is the Volcano event in Last Z: Survival Shooter?

It is the Volcano War (also called Volcano Battle): an event between war zones, split into attackers and defenders, who fight for one hour over the Black Ridge Volcano and the buildings around it. The side with the most faction points wins.

How does the Volcano War work?

First the sides are formed (announcement, communication, invitation and system allocation). Then come preparation, the combat hour and, finally, the distribution of the loot among players.

What do you do during the battle?

There are three ways to score: hold buildings, gather at the energy mines and defeat enemy troops inside the volcano area. When the battle starts, your headquarters is kicked out and you have to teleport back in.

How long does the event last?

The event runs over several days, but the battle itself lasts one hour.

What rewards does the Volcano War give?

Allocation chests with hero fragments from the current season, faction rewards for the whole side and individual ranking prizes down to rank 2000. Details are in the rewards section.

Is hard mode worth it?

Only the defender can activate it. In exchange for not being able to invite allies, it gets combat bonuses and a bigger prize pool. It is a good bet if your side trusts its strength; see hard mode for more.
